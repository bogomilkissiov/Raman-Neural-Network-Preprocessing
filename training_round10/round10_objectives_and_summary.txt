----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES OF ROUND 10
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Validate Architectural Findings on Fresh Synthetic Data:
   - Round 7, 8, and 9 were evaluated on `training_round7/testing_data7`.
   - Round 10 generates a brand-new training dataset (40,960 spectra) and a testing dataset
     doubled in size (10,240 spectra: 5 resolutions x 2,048 spectra) using identical physical
     generation parameters (polynomial degree up to 16, identical cosmic ray rates, noise levels,
     and peak distributions).
   - This prevents any risk of overfitting model design decisions to testing_data7.

2. Rigorous Multi-Seed Validation Across 5 Seeds:
   - Uses 5 unseen seeds: [300, 301, 302, 303, 304].
   - Increases sample statistical power (n=5 per model) to clearly distinguish true architectural
     superiority from random initialization swings.

3. Focused Length-Scalar Ablation:
   - Isolate the true impact of providing the normalized sequence length scalar (L / 1600.0)
     to the baseline polynomial regression head:
       * Removal test: V3.3 (with length) vs V3.3-NoLen (without length).
       * Does removing length knowledge degrade the 4-quadrant spatial pooling model?
   - The ablation is strictly paired per seed with identical weight initialization in all other
     layers and identical batch sequences during training.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 2. SUMMARY OF TESTED MODELS (5 MODELS TOTAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. V3.0 (Original Reference):
   - Parameters: 148,426
   - Baseline Trunk: 128-channel convolutional trunk with AdaptiveAvgPool1d(1) (1-bucket pool).
   - Baseline Head: 128 -> 128 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: NO.

2. V3.1 (Intermediate Re-architected Model):
   - Parameters: 104,554
   - Baseline Trunk: 32-channel trunk with strided stem + 3 dilated ResBlocks (251-bin RF).
   - Baseline Head: 33 -> 128 -> 64 -> 8 FC (artificial channel expansion).
   - Length Scalar: YES (32 + 1 = 33).

3. V3.2 (Conv Projection + Contracting Funnel):
   - Parameters: 104,746
   - Baseline Trunk: 32-channel trunk + 1x1 conv projection to 128 channels + 1-bucket pool.
   - Baseline Head: Clean contracting funnel 129 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: YES (128 + 1 = 129).

4. V3.3 (4-Quadrant Spatial Pooling + Funnel, WITH Length):
   - Parameters: 100,266
   - Baseline Trunk: 32-channel trunk + AdaptiveAvgPool1d(4) preserving spatial layout.
   - Flattened: 32 channels x 4 quadrants = 128 spatial features.
   - Baseline Head: Clean contracting funnel 129 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: YES (128 + 1 = 129).

5. V3.3-NoLen (V3.3 Ablation, WITHOUT Length):
   - Parameters: 100,202 (-64 weights)
   - Baseline Trunk: Identical 32-channel trunk + AdaptiveAvgPool1d(4) (128 features).
   - Baseline Head: Clean contracting funnel 128 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: NO (purely spatial pooling).
