 1. PRIMARY OBJECTIVES OF ROUND 11
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Validate PolyGaussNet V3.4 (Scale-Invariant Architecture):
   - PolyGaussNet V3.4 eliminates the sequence length scalar entirely while retaining the clean
     contracting funnel and lightweight 32-channel convolutional trunk with wide 251-bin receptive field.
   - Evaluates whether spatial pooling alone provides complete scale-invariance and matches or exceeds
     the performance of the V3.0 reference benchmark without injecting artificial length coordinates.

2. Benchmark 4-Section vs 8-Section Spatial Pooling:
   - Evaluates the impact of doubling the spatial pooling partitions:
       * 4 Partitions (V3.4-4sec): Macro-spatial zones [Left, Center-Left, Center-Right, Right]
         yielding 32 x 4 = 128 inputs to the funnel (100,202 parameters).
       * 8 Partitions (V3.4-8sec): Octant-level spatial sampling yielding 32 x 8 = 256 inputs
         to the funnel (108,394 parameters, +8,192 weights).
   - Core research hypothesis: Since the target baseline is a 7th-order polynomial (8 free coefficients),
     8 spatial partitions provide sufficient degrees of freedom to directly sample up to 7th-degree
     inflection points across the spectrum.

3. High-Power Statistical Validation Across 10 Unseen Seeds:
   - Uses 10 unseen seeds: [400, 401, 402, 403, 404, 405, 406, 407, 408, 409].
   - Tests 3 models x 10 seeds = 30 training runs total.
   - High statistical sample size (n=10) provides definitive confidence intervals and paired win-loss
     counts, extinguishing random seed variance from the architectural conclusion.

 2. SUMMARY OF TESTED MODELS (3 MODELS TOTAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. V3.0 (Original Reference Baseline):
   - Parameters: 148,426
   - Baseline Trunk: 128-channel conv trunk + AdaptiveAvgPool1d(1) (1-bucket pool).
   - Baseline Head: 128 -> 128 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: NO.

2. V3.4-4sec (V3.4 with 4-Section Spatial Pooling):
   - Parameters: 100,202 (-48,224 vs V3.0, -32.5%)
   - Baseline Trunk: 32-channel conv trunk + 3 dilated ResBlocks (251-bin RF) + AdaptiveAvgPool1d(4).
   - Flattened: 32 channels x 4 quadrants = 128 spatial features.
   - Baseline Head: Clean contracting funnel 128 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: NO.

3. V3.4-8sec (V3.4 with 8-Section Spatial Pooling):
   - Parameters: 108,394 (-40,032 vs V3.0, +8,192 vs V3.4-4sec)
   - Baseline Trunk: 32-channel conv trunk + 3 dilated ResBlocks (251-bin RF) + AdaptiveAvgPool1d(8).
   - Flattened: 32 channels x 8 octants = 256 spatial features.
   - Baseline Head: Clean contracting funnel 256 -> 64 -> 8 FC.
   - Length Scalar: NO.

- Checkpoint Selection:
  * Best training loss across epochs (testing set is strictly held-out until testing11.py runs).
